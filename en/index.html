When the product isn't enough.

A few situations to understand what no longer holds between people, usage and products, and what we can design differently.

1 / 5

Notifications everywhere, and teams that no longer read anything.

We tell ourselves
We need to target messages better.
What's really going on
What holds it together
???Attention: what we give to someone or something. Here, the tool scatters it across everything that arrives, and nothing keeps it on what matters.

Favors between colleagues replaced by a ticketing system.

We tell ourselves
It's clearer, and it's measurable.
What's really going on
What holds it together
???Value: everything given without a price, advice, time, a helping hand. Here, once it becomes something owed, it stops circulating.

A dashboard that measures who contributes the most, and contributions that drop.

We tell ourselves
We need better indicators, or a better reward.
What's really going on
What holds it together
???Recognition: being seen for what you bring. Here, the ranking replaces it with a score, and everyone only sees their own rank.

A community very active at launch, silent six months later.

We tell ourselves
The novelty has worn off.
What's really going on
What holds it together
???Obligation: the urge to give back what we received, which makes people return. Here, nothing lets them give back, so nobody comes back.

Everyone knows something is wrong, and nobody says it.

We tell ourselves
Teams lack courage, or commitment.
In the blind spot
What appears
???Trust: being able to speak without fearing you're the only one saying it. Here, everyone knows, but doesn't know that the others know too.
