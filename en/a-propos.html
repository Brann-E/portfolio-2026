12 years of design, and one more question.

For twelve years, I've designed platforms, dashboards, care pathways and sales tools, for groups like AXA, Novo Nordisk, Nexans or Orange, and for smaller organizations.

In recent years, I've deepened my practice through anthropology, systems thinking and reading about how people innovate, decide and work as a collective. These explorations led me to widen the question:

What allows an object to find its place, to be adopted, and to keep evolving?

How I work.

I work with designers, domain experts and technical teams to understand how things are used, bring different points of view together and give the product a direction.

Depending on the need, I run the inquiry, facilitate the exchanges or give shape to the options: journeys, interfaces, prototypes. I try to connect what we learn, what we decide and what we design, so that the work moves forward and everyone can contribute to it.
