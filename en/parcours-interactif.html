    A direction. Not a solution.

    The same thing happens elsewhere.

    3 times the same thing. One single mechanism.

    4 people give something that never had a name.
    43 reports written with nobody at the other end.
    A woman who gives 6 times without anything being taken up.

    Every time, something was circulating. Every time, nobody was receiving it.

    And every time, what was enough was tiny: a monthly message, a quarter of an hour, half a page.

    What was missing was never goodwill. It was a place where what circulates can arrive.

    This is what I do in the products I design. I make visible what already circulates, I repair the place where it doesn't arrive, and I design the tool that makes it hold.
