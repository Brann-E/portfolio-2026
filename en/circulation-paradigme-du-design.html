Circulation, the next paradigm of design?

Six texts, in the form of attempts, explorations.

Take what seems most solid to you: a team, an institution, a bond. It is not an object, it is a flow in which value, recognition and attention circulate permanently. Cut what passes through, and only an empty shell remains. Six texts follow this thread, from the most everyday design to what holds the world up.

If you could only read one, read this one.

Text 06 · the culmination · 9 min

What makes things circulate

The text where the whole series converges, which finally answers the question the other five left open: through what, concretely, does a relationship circulate? Through a medium - a tool, a convention, a ritual - and designing these media is precisely my job. The five texts below trace the path, this one is the destination.
